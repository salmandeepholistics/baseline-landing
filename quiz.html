FAQs

Before we call you

Is the health check and the call really free?

Yes. The 60-second check and the call with our health expert are free, with no payment and no obligation. You only pay if you decide to book Baseline.

Do I need to fast before the test?

Yes, for the first sample, which is taken before breakfast. The second sample is taken after breakfast. Your health expert will confirm the exact timings on your call.

Who collects my sample, and where?

Our NABL and CAP accredited partner lab collects both samples at your home, anywhere in Bengaluru. No centre visit needed.

When do I get my results?

Within 48 hours of collection, on your dashboard. Then we book your 1-on-1 doctor consultation.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. Baseline is for preventive insight and health optimisation. It complements your doctor; it doesn't replace them.
